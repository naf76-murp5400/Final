Mixed-Use Development Strategy

Capital Allocation & Public-Private Investment

Overview

This portfolio evaluates three mixed-use development opportunities: Waterfront DC, Landover Mall, and South Riding, VA.

The objective is to balance financial returns, risk, and public-private partnership opportunities.

Development Strategy

South Riding, VA

Flagship Investment

Highest NOI and land value. Primary return driver.

Waterfront DC

Urban Stabilizer

Lower cost, steady returns, strong planning alignment.

Landover Mall

Conditional Opportunity

Requires public-private partnership to unlock value.

Capital Allocation

  • 60–70% → South Riding (Return Engine)
  • 20–30% → Waterfront DC (Stability)
  • 10–20% → Landover Mall (Conditional)

Final Recommendation

Approve a phased mixed-use strategy led by South Riding, supported by Waterfront DC, and conditionally including Landover Mall through public-private partnership.
